## v1.1.0: Trusted Policy Delivery and Key Lifecycle Hardening

v1.1.0 strengthens the AOMQTT control plane by adding a policy trust layer on top of the v1.0.2 client-side policy guard.

### Main features

- Signing abstraction for local, remote, and mock-KMS policy signing
- `signer_key_id` based trust verification
- Key Revocation List (KRL) support
- Rejection of policies signed by unknown or revoked keys
- Rejection of invalid policy signatures
- Trusted control policy processor combining trust verification and policy safety checks
- Controller-side anomaly report generation
- Runtime trust scenario runner for release and paper evaluation

### Research positioning

v1.1.0 extends AOMQTT from encrypted and obfuscated MQTT delivery to a more trustworthy control-plane architecture. In addition to verifying policy signatures, clients can reject policies from unknown or revoked signing keys, preserve the last-known-good policy, and report traceable ACK/anomaly artifacts to the controller.
